\textbf{Key Modules:} Designed a local-global denoising module integrating SAM segmentation with Gaussian/OTSU multi-head filters to suppress pump-light noise patterns and global background interference.

\textbf{Experimental Results:} Built a multi-scenario benchmark with 7,200 airflow images and 1,440 combustion images; completed PyTorch training/validation and achieved MAE $\leq$ 3.17 m/s with max relative error $\leq$ 4\%, significantly outperforming existing methods.

\textbf{Engineering Delivery:} Packaged the algorithm for practical use and open-sourced the velocimetry software plus data acquisition demo on GitHub, attracting broad interest from aerospace and computer vision researchers.
